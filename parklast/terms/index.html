ParkLast Terms of Service

The terms for using ParkLast, the Android app that remembers where you parked.

Effective date: [PUBLICATION DATE] · Provider: Raanan Herscovici, an Israeli sole proprietorship (osek murshe) trading as Imavelo (“we”) · admin@imavelo.com

1. The service

ParkLast notices when your phone disconnects from the Bluetooth system of a car you set up and saves the parking location using your phone’s location services. You can then navigate back to the car with an external maps app.

2. What the service needs

Automatic saving depends on your phone and its settings:

  • Bluetooth on;
  • location permission set to “all the time”;
  • location services on;
  • the manufacturer’s battery and background restrictions;
  • GPS reception.

ParkLast tells you when a required setting is missing. Even so, there may be times when a location is not saved.

3. Location accuracy – no guarantee

  • ParkLast uses your phone’s location services. Inside buildings and underground car parks, location signals may be limited, so a saved location may be approximate rather than exact.
  • ParkLast does not detect the parking level.
  • We do not guarantee accuracy, availability, or that every parking will be saved.

4. Navigation through other services

  • Navigation opens a third-party maps app, such as Google Maps, with the saved coordinates.
  • The destination name it shows, the route and walking times are decided by that app and are outside our control.
  • Please walk and drive carefully and lawfully.

5. Paid subscription

  • The price, billing period and automatic renewal are shown by Google Play before you buy.
  • You can cancel at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you keep access until then.
  • “Restore purchases” in ParkLast’s settings restores an active subscription on the same Google account.
  • Without an active subscription, ParkLast does not save parking locations automatically and tells you so.
