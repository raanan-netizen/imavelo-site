Delete your ParkLast data

How to delete the data ParkLast keeps, and how to ask Imavelo to delete your subscription data.

App: ParkLast for Android (Google Play package com.parklast.app) · Provider: {{OPERATOR}} · {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}

In short

  • On your phone: you delete it yourself, at any time. We have no copy.
  • Subscription data at RevenueCat: e-mail {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} with your Support ID or a Google Play order number. We handle requests within 30 days.
  • Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play.
  • Payment records at Google: held by Google. We cannot delete them.

1. Data on your phone

Your parking locations, cars, event log and settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them, so only you can delete them:

  • Event log: Settings → Event log → Clear.
  • Everything: Android Settings → Apps → ParkLast → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall ParkLast.

Removing a car in ParkLast does not delete its last saved parking location. Use Clear storage or uninstall for that.

2. Subscription data at RevenueCat

ParkLast checks your subscription with RevenueCat, Inc., which keeps this data for us as a service provider (see the Privacy Policy, section 6):

  • a random app user ID created by the app, shown in ParkLast as your Support ID;
  • your purchase history and subscription status, if you bought a subscription;
  • the basic device and app information stored with them.

This record exists even if you never bought a subscription, because ParkLast asks RevenueCat whether one is active. It does not contain your name or e-mail address.

If your version of ParkLast shows no Support ID in Settings, that version does not use RevenueCat and has sent it no data.

3. How to ask us to delete it

  1. Cancel an active subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting your data does not cancel the subscription, and Google would keep charging you.
  2. Copy your Support ID: ParkLast → Settings → Support ID → Copy. Do this before uninstalling; the ID cannot be shown afterwards.
  3. Uninstall ParkLast on every phone where it is installed. If ParkLast is still installed, it contacts RevenueCat again the next time it opens and a new record is created.
  4. E-mail {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} with the subject “ParkLast data deletion” and one of these:
    • your Support ID, or
    • a Google Play order number for ParkLast. It starts with GPA. and appears in the receipt e-mail from Google and in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history.

Do not send passwords, card details or screenshots of your Google account. We only need the Support ID or the order number.

What we do: we find the record in RevenueCat, delete it, check that it can no longer be found, and confirm by e-mail within 30 days of your request. If we cannot find a record, we tell you within the same time and ask for the other identifier.

If you uninstalled ParkLast without copying the Support ID and never bought a subscription, we have no way to find your record. It contains no name or e-mail and is no longer updated after you uninstall.

4. What is deleted

The whole RevenueCat record for that Support ID: the random app user ID, purchase history, subscription status and the device and app information stored with them.

5. Data that may be kept

  • Google Play: Google processes the payment and keeps its own records of the order under the Google Privacy Policy. They are under Google’s control, and we cannot delete them. To ask Google, use your Google account.
  • Our financial records: the sales and payout reports Google sends us may include order numbers. We keep them for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Your request: we keep the e-mail correspondence about your request as a record that we handled it, and to deal with any dispute about it.

6. Restore backup in your Google account (Block Store)

After a purchase, the RevenueCat library may save the random app user ID in your Google account’s Block Store, so that you can restore your purchase if you reinstall ParkLast. ParkLast cannot remove that ID once the app is uninstalled. After we delete your RevenueCat record, the ID is no longer linked to any purchase data. If you later reinstall ParkLast, a new record is created, and restoring purchases adds your Google Play purchases to it again.
